Key ratios

Debt-to-asset
Total liabilities ÷ total assets. Under 30% is comfortable.
–
Liquidity runway
Cash and FDs ÷ monthly outgo (expenses + EMIs). 6 months or more is a good buffer.
–
Short-term debt cover
Cash and FDs ÷ short-term debt. Below 1× means you cannot clear short-term debt from savings.
–
Savings rate
Left over ÷ take-home income.
–
EMI burden
EMIs ÷ take-home income. Above 40% leaves little room.
–
Liquid share of assets
Cash and FDs ÷ total assets.
–
Risk asset share
Equity funds, stocks and crypto ÷ total assets. Above 50% is high.
–
Home loan-to-value
Home loan ÷ real estate value. Above 60% means most of the property is still owed.
–
Net worth in years of income
Net worth ÷ annual take-home income.
–

What this says

Liquid assets are cash and fixed deposits. EPF, gold and real estate are excluded from the runway. Thresholds are rules of thumb for a personal balance sheet, not advice.
